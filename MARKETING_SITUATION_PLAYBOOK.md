# Situation-led growth playbook

## Positioning

House of Eon should answer the question behind the perfume search, not compete
only for broad category terms. The unit of production is one situation:

`Search intent → short-form hook → useful answer → matched products → trial pack`

The promise must stay the same across search, Reel/Short, Meta ad and landing
page. Change the edit and format, not the claim.

## One-content production recipe

Use the landing page's “20-second answer” as the spoken script foundation.

1. **0–2 seconds — pattern break:** say the situation exactly as the audience
   experiences it.
2. **2–8 seconds — correct the common mistake:** explain why the usual choice or
   spray habit fails.
3. **8–18 seconds — give the rule:** name the scent direction and spray plan.
4. **18–25 seconds — show two choices:** one safe match and one alternative.
5. **Final frame — CTA:** “See the full answer” for cold traffic; “Try any three
   for ₹249” for retargeting.

Create three edits from the same footage: a 20–25 second Reel/Short, a 10–12
second Meta prospecting cut, and a 6-second retargeting reminder.

## Publishing cadence

- Monday: publish one landing page and its Reel/Short.
- Tuesday: run the 10–12 second paid cut to the matching landing page.
- Thursday: publish a reply video answering one comment from the same situation.
- Saturday: retarget page viewers with the trial-pack cut.

Start with Chennai weather, office AC, first date and expensive-under-₹1000.
They cover climate, daily use, emotion and price without competing against each
other.

## Measurement

Use `view_situation_page` with `situation_slug` and `search_intent`, plus
`situation_cta_click` with `cta_placement`, to compare traffic quality. Judge
each situation as its own mini funnel:

- landing-page view → product view;
- landing-page view → add to cart;
- landing-page view → trial-pack click;
- add to cart → purchase;
- cost per landing-page view and cost per purchase by situation.

Do not pick winners from click-through rate alone. Keep a situation when it
produces product exploration or trial-pack intent even if its raw traffic is
smaller than a broad keyword campaign.

## Guardrails

- Do not promise a fixed number of lasting hours; skin, weather and sprays vary.
- Do not promise compliments or attraction. Explain what tends to be noticeable.
- Keep “under ₹1000” tied to the active EON20 price and verify it at checkout.
- Avoid near-duplicate pages. Chennai weather and humid weather must retain
  different examples, copy and intent.
- Refresh product recommendations when formulas, price or availability change.
