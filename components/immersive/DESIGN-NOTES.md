# Beyond the ordinary — House of Eon

A mobile-first homepage and six product detail pages, built on the existing Next.js storefront.

## Experience

- Obsidian, warm ivory and amber; oversized sans-serif with italic editorial accents.
- Generated cinematic Desert Tonka eclipse campaign, kept distinct from original product photographs.
- Pointer-responsive perspective imagery, kinetic type, scroll-linked text reveal, a pause control and reduced-motion support.
- An illustrative CSS 3D bottle with draggable rotation, keyboard range input and reset. This is a form study, not a scanned product model. Original product photography remains available in Product view.
- Scent-family filters, interactive mood matching, fragrance evolution tabs and verified-order reviews from the existing catalog.
- Live inventory guards, single/duo selection, cart-aware bundle pricing, add-to-bag feedback and sticky checkout actions.
- Existing checkout, payment, order tracking, support and return-policy routes are retained.

## Campaign asset

Built-in ImageGen was used. Reference: `public/products/desert-tonka.png`.

Project assets: `public/campaign/desert-eclipse.png` (original) and `public/campaign/desert-eclipse.webp` (193,604-byte web version).

### Exact generation prompt

Use case: compositing. Asset type: cinematic luxury perfume website campaign photo, landscape 1536x1024. Use the supplied House of Eon Desert Tonka product photo as the exact bottle identity reference. Preserve its bottle silhouette, rectangular clear amber glass, textured cylindrical gold cap, cream label, horse illustration and readable HOUSE OF EON / DESERT TONKA label. Replace the desert environment with a futuristic black obsidian studio, one dramatically lit amber bottle suspended slightly tilted clockwise over a polished dark black volcanic stone slab. An enormous softly luminous amber solar eclipse ring behind the bottle, subtle amber atmospheric haze and sculptural caustic light reflections on the glass. Composition: bottle centered around 58% horizontal and 52% vertical, occupies 65% of image height; breathing room on all sides, edges almost pure black #080908 so image can blend into dark UI. Extremely refined photoreal commercial fragrance photography, sharp product, dramatic rim lighting, deep shadows, warm metal highlights, subtle film grain, 85mm lens. No extra bottles, no added typography outside the actual product label, no decorative copy, no watermarks. Original surreal campaign art while preserving the recognizable real product.
